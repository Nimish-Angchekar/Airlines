# Key Insights

* Analyzed **5.8M flights across 14 airlines** in 2015, with an overall **80.9% on-time rate**.
* **1.54% of flights were cancelled**, while approximately **17.6% were delayed**.
* **Southwest Airlines** recorded the highest flight volume at approximately **1.26M flights**.
* **July** had the highest flight volume, while **February** recorded the lowest.
* **Late aircraft delays** were the largest contributor to total delays, followed by **airline-related delays**.
* Average departure delay was **9.37 minutes**, compared with **4.41 minutes** for arrivals.
* **ATL** was the busiest origin airport, handling approximately **347K flights**.
* Airport performance varied significantly, with some major hubs experiencing higher average delays and cancellation rates.
* Delay patterns varied by **airline, month, day of week, and airport**, highlighting operational hotspots.
* The analysis indicates that improving **aircraft turnaround and airline operations** could have a significant impact on reducing overall delays.

### Overall Takeaway

The analysis highlights a large-scale airline network with **strong overall on-time performance**, but delays remain primarily driven by **late aircraft and airline operational factors**, with performance varying considerably across airlines, airports, and time periods.
